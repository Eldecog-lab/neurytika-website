TWO CONNECTED ENGINEERING BRANCHES

Build the aircraft.
Bring intelligence closer to the data.

Aerospace puts practical constraints at the center of engineering: mass, energy, operating conditions and the information available when a decision is needed.

Our aircraft and AI activities meet around those constraints. Pneumatic structures address the physical side; lightweight, inspectable computation addresses how data can become a useful signal. Neurytika brings that same emphasis on local processing and clear evidence to enterprise prediction projects.

01 / AEROSPACE

Electric aircraft with pneumatic wings.

Celeste’s dAS20 program uses a pressurized textile wing, with aerodynamic lift and electric propulsion. The current prototype has an 8 m wingspan, and the company records its first take-off on 7 May 2026 at Le Havre airport.

WHY EMBEDDED AI IS A STRATEGIC BRANCH

Useful intelligence.
Right where the data is produced.

For an autonomous drone, processing sensor information onboard can help turn measurements into timely mission signals while making deliberate use of computing power, energy and connectivity.

That is why embedded AI is central to Celeste’s development direction. ERC’s one-pass learning approach and explicit evidence connect hardware-conscious computation with a model that engineers can inspect. The same engine provides the foundation for Neurytika’s explainable prediction workflow.

Environmental observation

Classify structured environmental measurements into an alert category and prioritize observations for an operator to review during a monitoring mission.

⚙

Equipment-state monitoring

Combine recorded vibration, temperature or electrical measurements to identify an operating-state category and provide context for engineering review.

Mission information filtering
