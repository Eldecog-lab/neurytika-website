What data do we start with?

A customer or account identifier, dated activity and service records, and a historical outcome such as renewed or cancelled. We define a prediction date and an outcome window so each record reflects information available when your team would act.

Define the outcome

For example: “Will this account cancel within the agreed period?” The target is a defined category. Your team selects the intervention and records what happens next.

Evaluate the business workflow

Review how many future departures are identified, how many retained customers are referred, and how the referral list fits your team’s capacity. A pilot uses held-out records and a time-based evaluation where the workflow calls for it.

What is included in a customer retention software proposal?

We scope the software license and implementation around your customer data, prediction objective, users and environment. The proposal can include data preparation, model evaluation, CRM workflow integration, deployment, training and support.

How can we reduce customer churn with our existing data?

Begin with a clear cancellation or renewal outcome and historical customer records. Neurytika learns patterns associated with that outcome, then classifies new records. Your team can examine the supporting evidence and decide which retention action fits each account.

Is this customer retention software or a CRM?

Neurytika provides the prediction and explanation workflow. We scope integration with your customer records and existing CRM so your team can use the outputs alongside its current tools.

Can we understand why a customer is flagged?

Yes. Your team can inspect the feature combinations contributing to the predicted category, along with their support in the training data. These associations provide context for review and customer conversations.

Can customer data stay on our infrastructure?

Yes. Neurytika can run locally within your organization. We also offer implementation in your preferred cloud environment.
