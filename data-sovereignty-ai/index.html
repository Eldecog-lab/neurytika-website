FROM SOVEREIGN AI STRATEGY TO A CONCRETE PROJECT

Make control
a deployment decision.

A sovereign AI strategy brings data, models, infrastructure and operations into the same conversation. For a prediction project, start with where the records are processed, who operates the environment and how the results are used.

Neurytika supports that approach through local processing and a workflow built around your structured data. We work with your business and IT teams to define the environment, integration and responsibilities for your project.

Processing location

Choose your own infrastructure or an agreed hosting environment. Define where datasets, models, results and backups belong.

Operating arrangements

Agree who manages the installation, which users need access and how support, updates and maintenance fit your organization’s processes.

Data flows

Map the records entering the model and the predictions returning to your systems. Include interfaces and any agreed support exchanges in the project scope.

Visible model evidence

Inspect feature combinations, support counts and contributing patterns so analysts and domain experts can examine the basis of a prediction.

Your deployment
questions, answered.

Can Neurytika run without a cloud service?

Yes. Neurytika can be installed on your infrastructure to process structured records locally. We scope the installation and integration with your IT team.

What does data sovereignty mean for a prediction project?

It means defining the authority and requirements governing your data, together with choices about processing location, operators and data flows. We translate your organization’s deployment requirements into a concrete software and implementation scope.

How does Neurytika support a sovereign AI strategy?

It gives your organization a local deployment option for prediction on tabular data, with models and explanations in the chosen environment. Your project defines hosting, access arrangements, operations and integration with your teams.

Can we use a hosting provider selected by our organization?

Yes. We can scope a cloud deployment in your preferred environment. Your organization sets the provider and operating requirements, and we define the software installation and integration work around that choice.

What makes the prediction inspectable?

Neurytika uses the Evidence Ratio Classifier (ERC). Your team can inspect contributing feature combinations and their statistical support in the training data. Read the open-access research paper for the method and published experiments.
