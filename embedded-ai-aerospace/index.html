THREE PRACTICAL STARTING POINTS

Make each measurement useful.

⌁

Drone & aircraft equipment health

Use battery, propulsion or vibration features to classify recorded operating states. Give maintenance and engineering teams the contributing signals behind an inspection priority.

Example: identify a battery operating-state category from temperature, voltage and current summaries paired with historical inspection labels.

◈

Satellite telemetry triage

Classify telemetry windows into defined system-state categories and attach the observed evidence. Help operators focus their review on the events that match their mission priorities.

Example: route a power-subsystem event to the right engineering review using prepared electrical and thermal features.

↗

Payload data & event prioritization

Classify prepared measurement records by quality or operational interest. Make the criteria behind a record’s priority available to the mission team and its data workflow.

Example: prioritize records for engineering review or transmission using known observation-quality labels.

A CLEAR DIRECTION FOR THE SECTOR

Public programmes show the need.

Onboard assessment, interpretable models and greater autonomy are already concrete research and engineering priorities.

NASA / JPL — OUTLAST

JPL developed onboard health classification from flight telemetry and event logs for future autonomous multi-flight operations. Its OUTLAST software flew aboard Ingenuity in January 2024.

Read the JPL project ↗

ESA / Airbus — ORBIT-STAR

ESA describes an Airbus demonstrator that analyses Columbus subsystem telemetry for fault detection and prognosis. Onboard execution is identified as a next step for future missions.

Read the ESA programme ↗

WHY NEURYTIKA

An explained category. A practical integration.

ERC learns combinations of structured features associated with target classes. This provides an engineering basis for inspecting the evidence behind a classification and discussing it with domain experts.

Develop the model with representative historical data, then engineer and measure the inference integration on your chosen onboard computer. The pilot defines memory, processing time, interfaces and the operator’s use of each result.

Define the useful category

Choose one equipment state or event category, its user and the moment when the result is needed. Map sensors, prepared features and historical labels.

03

Integrate & measure onboard
