How do we detect fraud
in our own records?

Start with structured transactions, insurance claims or administrative files and the outcomes of previous investigations. We define the review category, prepare the records and evaluate a model around the point at which your team would act.

For banking teams, this can mean identifying transactions for additional review. For insurers, it can mean flagging claims for investigation. For administrations, it can mean prioritizing files for control. Each referral brings contributing patterns and their observed support into the reviewer’s workflow.

Agree the evaluation around the review process: identified fraud cases, referrals that prove legitimate and the number of files the team can investigate. We scope a pilot around your records and review capacity.

answered.

What should we compare when choosing fraud detection software?

Start with your transaction or case data, the outcome you need to identify, the explanations investigators need and the review capacity of your team. A Neurytika pilot evaluates the model on your records and scopes integration and deployment around that workflow.

What data do we need?

Structured historical records with known outcomes are a useful starting point. We help identify the fields, labels and preparation needed for your fraud detection project.

What does the explanation show?

The combinations of features contributing to a prediction, alongside their observed support and association with classes in the training data.

Who makes the final assessment?

Your investigators review the proposed case and its supporting evidence. We shape the software workflow around their expertise and your internal process.

Can fraud data stay on our infrastructure?

Yes. Neurytika can be installed locally so datasets and models remain in your environment. We also support a cloud setup if you prefer.
