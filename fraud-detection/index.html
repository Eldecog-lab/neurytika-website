Your questions,
answered.

What data do we need?

Structured historical records with known outcomes are a useful starting point. We help identify the fields, labels and preparation needed for your fraud detection project.

What does the explanation show?

The combinations of features contributing to a prediction, alongside their observed support and association with classes in the training data.

Who makes the final assessment?

Your investigators review the proposed case and its supporting evidence. We shape the software workflow around their expertise and your internal process.

Can fraud data stay on our infrastructure?

Yes. Neurytika can be installed locally so datasets and models remain in your environment. We also support a cloud setup if you prefer.
