THE DIFFERENCE, AT A GLANCE

What do you want AI to produce?

Both approaches learn patterns from data. Their practical distinction is the job you give them and the output you evaluate.

Generative AI and predictive AI in business workflows
Your questionGenerative AIPredictive AI
Main outputNew content: a reply, summary, image or draft.An estimated outcome, score, value or category.
Typical inputsInstructions and context, such as documents or conversations.Records with useful features and, for supervised learning, historical outcomes.
Business question“Can you summarize this report?”“Which outcome is associated with these record attributes?”
EvaluationUsefulness, factual accuracy and suitability of the generated content.Performance against held-out outcomes and usefulness in the operational workflow.
ExplanationA generated explanation can help communicate a result; its grounding should be assessed.Explanations depend on the model. Neurytika exposes contributing feature combinations and their statistical support.
DeploymentLocal or cloud, depending on the chosen model and implementation.Local or cloud, depending on the chosen model and implementation.

These are practical categories: language models can also perform classification, and predictive systems can use text-derived features. Choose and evaluate the complete workflow around your objective.

Is generative AI the same as predictive AI?

They share machine-learning foundations and can overlap in capability. In business use, generative AI is typically used to create content, while predictive AI estimates outcomes or assigns categories from data.

Can a language model classify records too?

Yes. Language models can classify records, including text. The choice depends on your data, target, evaluation results and explanation needs. Neurytika offers a dedicated approach for structured-data classification with inspectable feature combinations and statistical support.

What records do we need to begin?

Start with structured historical records and known outcomes, such as completed inspections, resolved incidents or reviewed cases. We help define useful features, target categories and an evaluation plan for your project.

Does explainable AI mean a generated explanation?

With Neurytika, the explanation exposes feature combinations and statistical associations used by ERC. These elements give your team something concrete to inspect alongside the prediction.

Can our data stay inside our organization?

Yes. Neurytika can be deployed on your infrastructure so datasets and models remain in your environment. A cloud deployment is also available when that fits your preferred setup.
