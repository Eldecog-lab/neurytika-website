BEFORE WE TALK

Your questions, answered

A clear starting point
for your project.

Is Neurytika software or a consulting service?

Both. We provide the prediction software and can help prepare your data, adapt and evaluate the model, integrate the workflow, deploy it and train your team.

Can our data stay in our organization?

Yes. Neurytika can be deployed on your infrastructure and process data locally, with no cloud service required. Cloud deployment is also available.

What should we bring to the first conversation?

Your prediction objective, an overview of your tabular data and your preferred deployment environment. You can describe the project before sharing any dataset.

How is a commercial project scoped?

We discuss your use case, data, integration and deployment needs, then define the software and consulting scope with you. Contact us for a proposal.

How do we get academic access?

Students, teachers and researchers can register through the academic access form. Include your institution and intended educational or non-commercial research use.
