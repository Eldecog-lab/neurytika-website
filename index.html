THE TECHNOLOGY BEHIND THE SOFTWARE

04 — Powered by ERC

Make the pattern
part of the explanation.

ERC represents evidence as combinations of discrete features, with counts and probabilities that can be inspected directly.

Numeric values are grouped into bins and categorical values become tokens. ERC evaluates combinations up to a configurable size and tracks their support across classes. These local evidence units connect a prediction to concrete patterns in the dataset.

01

See which features occur together

Inspect meaningful combinations of discrete features and the outcomes associated with them.

02

Examine the evidence

Read support counts alongside conditional probabilities to understand the observations behind a pattern.

03

Trace a prediction

Inspect activated patterns, their weights and their support to understand the evidence contributing to a selected prediction.
