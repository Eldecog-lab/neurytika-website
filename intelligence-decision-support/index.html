Evidence you can inspect. Expertise you can work with.

Neurytika is developed by Celeste EcoFlyers in France. Its Evidence Ratio Classifier (ERC) represents relationships between discrete feature combinations and target classes, making their statistical support available for inspection. The research is published in Neurocomputing.
