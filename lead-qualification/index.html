Structured prospect and account fields, dated activity summaries and historical outcomes such as converted or not converted within an agreed window. We help select fields available before the decision point and define how new records reach the model.

How do we evaluate a lead scoring tool before deployment?

We define the conversion outcome and the data available at qualification, then evaluate predicted categories on held-out records. The pilot compares the result with your existing qualification process and considers how many leads your team can follow up.

How do we identify the best leads to contact?

Start with your definition of a successful sales outcome and the information available at qualification. We use your historical records to build and evaluate a classification model, then help integrate its output into your follow-up workflow.

How does this relate to lead scoring?

Lead scoring helps teams decide where to focus. Neurytika supports that goal through predicted outcome categories and inspectable evidence. We agree the priority categories and their use in your sales process during the project.

Does Neurytika find new prospects?

Neurytika works with the prospect records and historical outcomes you provide. We help your team qualify and prioritize that existing pipeline.

Can we use our CRM data locally?

Yes. We can scope a local workflow using structured CRM records and an installation in your environment, or deploy in your chosen cloud.
