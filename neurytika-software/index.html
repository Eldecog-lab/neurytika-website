Questions to ask before choosing your AI software

How can an expert understand and challenge a recommendation?

Neurytika makes the feature combinations and statistical evidence supporting a classification inspectable. Your expert can review the inputs, add missing context and decide what action to take. The pilot evaluates whether these explanations are useful to your reviewers.

See the review workflow

Can the model learn progressively without retraining on all past data?

ERC updates its counters from new labelled observations. For a deployed workflow, agree who validates outcomes and when model updates are released. An expert’s disagreement initiates a review; a validated outcome supplies the learning example.

Read the ERC paper

Can we keep confidential data on our own infrastructure?

Yes. Celeste offers on-premise deployment or deployment in your chosen cloud. We scope data flows, access permissions, retention and operational responsibilities with your team before implementation.

Explore deployment choices

How does this help people affected by a decision?

Inspectable evidence gives your organization a basis for explaining a proposal, checking a disputed input and organizing reconsideration. We help adapt explanations to their audience and define a meaningful human review process, using CNIL guidance as a public reference.

CNIL: explainability

What will we see in the demonstration?

An engineer reviews an investment proposal for a power grid, examines supporting information, adds renovation context and records a reasoned decision. A final step illustrates learning from a validated outcome. The demonstration uses fictional data and scripted proposals; the pilot evaluates the software on your own records.

How do we start, and what does the pilot deliver?

Choose one decision your team needs to explain, a representative data sample and a domain expert. Together we agree the evaluation protocol, prediction-quality criteria, explanation review, deployment requirements and deliverables. Request a scoped proposal for your project.

Discuss a technology evaluation

What makes ERC’s explanations intrinsic?

ERC represents learned evidence as explicit combinations of feature values and associated counts. Neurytika exposes matching patterns for inspection, linking the explanation to the model’s own representation. This helps an expert examine the statistical basis of a proposal and the inputs that deserve verification.

Research: ERC in Neurocomputing

The open-access paper “Evidence ratio classifier: A one-pass model for fast incremental learning” describes the method and evaluates it on Adult Income, Mushroom, Iris and Credit-Card Fraud. Use these published experiments to examine the method, and your pilot to measure its value on your own data.
