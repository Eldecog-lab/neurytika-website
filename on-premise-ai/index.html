What do you need to prepare a local deployment proposal?

Describe the prediction objective, the structured records available, intended users and the environment where the software should run. We review deployment, data flows and integration with your IT team, then define a tailored software and implementation scope.

Does prediction require a cloud service?

Neurytika can process data locally on your infrastructure, with no cloud service required for that prediction workflow.

Can we choose cloud deployment instead?

Yes. We can work with your team on a deployment in your preferred cloud environment, configured around your data flows and access requirements.

What infrastructure do we need?

We size the setup with your team based on the dataset, model configuration, expected workload and integration needs. Describe your environment in the contact form to begin.

What type of AI does Neurytika provide?

Neurytika is explainable prediction software for structured, tabular data. It learns feature combinations to classify observations and show supporting evidence.
