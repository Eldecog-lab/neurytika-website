Evaluate the business workflow

How does Neurytika fit our existing quality management system?

Neurytika provides classification and explanation for structured production records. We scope how its predictions and evidence fit your current inspection and quality workflows, including data preparation and integration with your existing tools.

How can production data help improve quality control?

Past production attributes, measurements and inspection outcomes can be used to learn patterns associated with quality categories. Neurytika applies the model to new structured records and makes contributing patterns visible for review.

What kind of data does Neurytika use?

Tabular records with structured fields and an outcome category. For quality projects, these can include measurements, batch attributes and historical inspection results, prepared around your prediction objective.

Can this help us reduce product defects?

It can support a workflow that identifies records for additional checks and gives engineers evidence to investigate. We define the quality objective and evaluate the predictions with your team as part of the project.

Can it run inside our factory IT environment?

Yes. We scope local deployment, data preparation and integration with your internal workflow. A cloud setup is also available.
