Five ways to keep your organization in control

Understand the proposal

Examine feature combinations, their observed counts and their association with outcomes. Bring the model’s evidence into a conversation with your domain experts.

Keep the final decision

Design a workflow in which experts can add context, disagree and explain their choice. Our implementation support helps turn human oversight into an effective review step.

Learn from validated feedback

ERC supports incremental learning: new labelled observations update its counts without restarting the full training process. Define who validates feedback and when updates enter service.

Make decisions open to review

Use inspectable evidence to support explanations for reviewers and people affected by a decision. Shape understandable information, correction and reconsideration procedures with your team.

Control your data

Deploy locally or in your chosen cloud. Agree data selection, access, retention and model-update responsibilities as part of the implementation.

Public guidance and research

PUBLIC GUIDANCE

CNIL: explanations people can understand

The CNIL defines explainability as making the elements behind an AI result understandable, with explanations adapted to their audience. Its guidance on AI in production addresses effective human oversight and the quality of continuous-learning data.

CNIL — Explainability ↗CNIL — Human oversight & AI in production ↗

Défenseur des droits — meaningful human involvement

Its 2024 report calls for meaningful human intervention and effective transparency in public services. We use these questions to frame the pilot: can the reviewer understand, challenge and reconsider a proposal?

ERC — the scientific basis

The open-access Neurocomputing paper describes learning through counter updates, inspectable evidence and reproducibility. Sections 7.2, 10.3 and 10.4 support the discussion of incremental learning and auditability.

Read the source ↗

Human–AI research — preserve expert autonomy

A CHI 2020 study observed professionals overriding erroneous algorithmic recommendations in a specific decision-support setting. It supports evaluating the human–AI workflow as well as the model.

Read the source ↗

Transparency matters to the people affected

Public debates about welfare, employment and tax controls highlight questions about data selection, unequal impacts and the ability to challenge a decision. La Quadrature du Net questions both the transparency and the purposes of these systems. These investigations help define the questions an organization should examine before deployment.

  • France Travail — profiling and oversight (2024)
  • CAF — analysis of the scoring system (2023)
  • Tax controls — arguments on effective human oversight (2019)

Explainability provides a basis for examining fairness. Our consulting helps teams define appropriate objectives, review data and errors, and organize meaningful human review.

A CONTROLLED LEARNING LOOP

Your expertise becomes a lasting contribution.

  1. Understand

    Review the evidence behind the proposal.

  2. Discuss

    Add context, correct inputs and examine alternatives.

  3. Decide

    The expert records the choice and its rationale.

  4. Enrich

    A separately validated outcome can update the model. A disagreement first triggers review; feedback enters learning through an agreed validation process.
